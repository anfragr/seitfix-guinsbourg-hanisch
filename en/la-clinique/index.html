The clinic

A Haussmann-era apartment on avenue Marceau, fitted out as a dental practice.

Entrance and reception

A large double door opens onto a marble floor and the reception desk, where our team will greet you and arrange your appointments.

The waiting room

Mouldings, parquet flooring and daylight: a calm room to wait in, away from the treatment rooms.

The corridor

It leads to the treatment rooms. The artworks and light fittings give the corridor its character.

The treatment rooms

Full of light, and equipped for each dentist’s area of work, including one with an operating microscope.

The operating microscope

The hygiene rooms

Reserved for gum care, scaling and periodontal maintenance.

Periodontology

The operating room

A room reserved for surgery, separate from the treatment rooms, used for implants, grafts and endodontic surgery.

The operating room

The recovery room

After a procedure, you can rest here before you leave.

Ac­cess­ib­il­ity

The practice has a treatment room and toilet facilities adapted for people with reduced mobility.

To help your visit go smoothly, please let us know about any particular needs when you book.
