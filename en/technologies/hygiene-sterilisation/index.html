Hygiene and ster­il­isa­tion

How instruments are cleaned and sterilised, in line with the hygiene rules that apply to dental practices.

Instrument de­con­tam­in­a­tion

  1. 1Pre-disinfectionAfter treatment, the instruments are immersed in a detergent-disinfectant solution.
  2. 2CleaningThey are cleaned, rinsed and dried.
  3. 3PackagingThey are placed in sealed pouches.
  4. 4SterilisationThe pouches are sterilised in an autoclave using pressurised steam.

Each sterilisation cycle is checked and recorded, so that the instruments can be traced.

Single-use items

Some items, such as needles, cups, protective covers and drapes, are single-use and discarded after each patient.

Between patients

The dental chair, work surfaces and equipment are disinfected between patients.
