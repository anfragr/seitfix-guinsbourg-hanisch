Technology

Imaging

From targeted X-rays to three-dimensional imaging.

Types of imaging

Periapical X-ray
A small image of one to three teeth, used to check for decay, during root canal treatment or at a check-up.
Panoramic X-ray
An overview of both jaws and all the teeth.
3D imaging (cone beam CT)
Three-dimensional cross-sections of the bone, used to plan implants, some surgical procedures and some root canal treatments.

Depending on what is needed, your dentist will prescribe an X-ray or 3D imaging, carried out at the practice or at an imaging centre. Only examinations that are useful for your diagnosis or treatment are prescribed.

Three-dimensional imaging

Before an implant is placed, 3D imaging shows the volume of available bone and the position of the nerves and sinuses. It serves as the basis for planning and, where appropriate, for making a surgical guide.

Radiation doses

Dental X-rays use low doses of radiation. 3D imaging involves a higher dose than a conventional X-ray, so it is only prescribed when it provides useful information.

If you are pregnant or think you may be, please tell us before any examination.
