Technology

The operating microscope

A larger, clearer view for more precise work.

The principle

The operating microscope is an optical instrument that magnifies and illuminates the working area in the mouth.

It is mounted on an articulated arm above the dental chair. Your dentist looks through the eyepieces, with magnification adjusted to the task. Lighting aligned with the line of sight reduces shadows.

What is it used for?

  • In endodonticsLocating very fine canals, removing old filling materials, detecting a crack.
  • In surgeryCarrying out delicate procedures, for example during endodontic surgery.
  • In prosthodonticsChecking the precision of preparations and the fit of crown margins.

For you

Better visibility allows more precise work that preserves more tooth tissue. For you, nothing changes: you sit in the dental chair just as you would for routine treatment.
