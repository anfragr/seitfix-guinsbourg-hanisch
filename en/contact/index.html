In an emergency

If you are in pain or have swelling, call the practice. On Sundays and public holidays, an out-of-hours dental service operates across the Île-de-France region: request help via urgences-dentaires.org. In a life-threatening emergency, call 15 (SAMU, the emergency medical service) or 112 (the European emergency number).
