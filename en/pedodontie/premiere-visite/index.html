Paediatric dentistry

The first visit

When to come, how to prepare your child and what happens at the appointment.

At what age?

A first visit is recommended at around the age of one, or in the months after the first teeth appear.

Coming early, before there is any problem, lets your child discover the practice on a positive note, and gives you advice suited to their age.

Preparing your child

  • Talk about the appointment simply and positively
  • Avoid words that might frighten, such as “needle” or “hurt”
  • Do not promise that “the dentist won’t do anything”
  • Read a book about it or play dentists at home
  • Choose a time when your child is well rested

The procedure

  1. 1Getting to knowYour child gets to know the chair, the light and the instruments.
  2. 2ExaminationThe dentist examines the teeth and gums, sometimes with the youngest children sitting on a parent’s lap.
  3. 3AdviceBrushing, toothpaste, diet, dummies: parents’ questions are welcome.
  4. 4Next stepsThe next check-up is booked, usually for a year’s time.

Your role as a parent

Staying calm yourself helps to reassure your child. With the youngest children, having you there during the examination often helps. Your dentist will explain how best to support your child.

Frequently asked questions

Can I stay with my child?

In most cases yes, especially for the youngest children.

What if my child cries?

This is common and nothing to worry about. The appointment is adjusted to suit your child, who will come back more relaxed next time.
