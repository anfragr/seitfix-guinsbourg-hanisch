Treatments

The practice brings together five areas of care. Each treatment is carried out by the dentist trained in that area, and cases that involve more than one area are discussed as a team.

  • Implantology
  • Aesthetic prosthodontics
  • Endodontics
  • Periodontology
  • Paediatric dentistry

Implantology

An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth. It then supports a crown, a bridge or a full-arch prosthesis. Placement is usually planned using three-dimensional imaging.

Dr Oliver Hanisch

Treatments

  • Dental implants
  • The implant procedure
  • Bone graft and sinus lift
  • Guided surgery
Learn more about implantology

Aesthetic pros­tho­dontics

Prosthodontics restores the shape, function and natural appearance of damaged or missing teeth. Crowns, veneers and bridges are custom-made, most often in ceramic.

Dr Olivier Guinsbourg, Dr Oliver Hanisch

Treatments

  • Ceramic crowns
  • Veneers
  • Bridges
  • Implant-supported prostheses
Learn more about aesthetic prosthodontics

Endodontics

When the pulp (the nerve) of a tooth is inflamed or infected, root canal treatment can usually save the tooth. Your dentist may use an operating microscope, which makes very fine canals visible.

Dr Sandrine Dahan

Treatments

  • Root canal treatment
  • Retreatment
  • Endodontic surgery
Learn more about endodontics

Peri­od­on­to­logy

Periodontitis affects the gums and the bone that support the teeth. Left untreated, it can cause the teeth to loosen and eventually fall out. Treatment aims to stop the infection, and regular follow-up then helps to maintain the result.

Treatments

  • Periodontal disease
  • Periodontal treatment
  • Gum grafts
  • Maintenance and hygiene
Learn more about periodontology

Paediatric dentistry

Baby teeth matter: they hold the space for the adult teeth. Care is adapted to the child’s age, with time taken to explain each step.

Dr Sophie de Scorraille

Treatments

  • The first visit
  • Caring for baby teeth
  • Prevention
  • Advice for parents
Learn more about paediatric dentistry
