Endodontics

Treating the inside of a tooth so that it can be saved rather than replaced.

Dr Sandrine DahanEndodontics

Book an appointment 01 45 74 66 56

What is endodontics?

Endodontics is the treatment of the pulp, the living tissue at the heart of the tooth that contains its nerves and blood vessels.

If the pulp becomes inflamed or infected as a result of deep decay, a fracture or a knock, root canal treatment allows the tooth to be saved. Left untreated, the infection can spread to the bone around the root and cause an abscess.

Warning signs

  • Sharp pain with hot or cold that lingers
  • Pain that comes on by itself, sometimes at night
  • Pain when biting
  • Swelling of the gum or cheek
  • A tooth that darkens after a knock

The practice has an operating microscope, which is used for root canal treatment. Magnification and lighting help to locate very fine canals, clean them along their full length and check each stage of the work.

Frequently asked questions

Is root canal treatment painful?

Usually not, as it is done under local anaesthetic. Your tooth may feel tender when you bite for a few days afterwards.

How many appointments will I need?

In most cases one or two. A severe infection may need an extra appointment.

Is a root-filled tooth more fragile?

Yes, particularly molars and premolars, which have often lost a lot of tooth structure. A crown is then often recommended to protect the tooth from fracture.

Is it covered by health insurance?

Root canal treatment is covered by the French national health insurance (Assurance maladie). The practice will explain the reimbursement terms before treatment begins.

Toothache?

Call the practice. Sharp pain or swelling should be seen promptly.
