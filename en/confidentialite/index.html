Privacy policy

How your data is processed when you use this website.

This translation is provided for information only. The French version is the legally binding one.

Data controller

Specialist dental practice of Drs Guinsbourg-Hanisch & Associés, 10, avenue Marceau, 75008 Paris.
Contact for data protection matters: [email address]

Data collected

When you send an appointment request, the practice receives the information you enter: surname, first name, phone number, email address, type of appointment, preferred time and language.

The form does not ask for any medical information. Please do not include any: you will be able to discuss it at your appointment.

Purpose and legal basis

This data is used solely to contact you to arrange an appointment. Processing is based on your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form and may withdraw at any time.

Retention period

Requests are deleted once they have been dealt with, and no later than one year after they were sent. If you become a patient of the practice, your records are then kept in accordance with the rules that apply to medical records.

Recipients

Your data is intended solely for the practice. The website and the form are hosted by Netlify, Inc., which processes the data on behalf of the practice. As this provider is established in the United States, the transfer is covered by the European Commission’s adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework), under which Netlify, Inc. is certified.

Your data is neither sold nor used for advertising purposes.

Cookies

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Fonts and videos are hosted on the website itself.

The “Open in Google Maps” link and other external links lead to third-party websites, which are subject to their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing and to data portability, as well as the right to withdraw your consent. To exercise these rights, write to [email address].

You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (Commission nationale de l’informatique et des libertés, the French data protection authority): cnil.fr.
