Aesthetic prosthodontics

Ceramic crowns

When a crown is needed, the materials available and how it is fitted.

What is a crown?

A crown is a cap that fully covers the visible part of a damaged tooth. It restores its shape, strength and appearance.

The crown is custom-made, then cemented or bonded onto the prepared tooth. If the tooth is missing, a crown can also be fixed onto an implant.

When is it used?

  • A badly damaged toothExtensive decay or a fracture that can no longer be repaired with a simple filling.
  • A root-filled toothAfter root canal treatment, a molar or premolar becomes more fragile and is often protected with a crown or an onlay (a partial crown).
  • A misshapen or heavily discoloured toothWhen a veneer cannot correct the shape or shade sufficiently.
  • Replacing an old crownWorn, fractured or unsightly.

Materials

Porcelain fused to metal
Ceramic fired onto a metal framework. Strong and well proven, but a dark line may show at the gum margin over time.
Zirconia
A very strong, metal-free ceramic, also suitable for molars.
Lithium disilicate
A metal-free, translucent ceramic, often chosen for front teeth.

The choice depends on where the tooth is, how much chewing force it has to withstand and the look you want.

The procedure

  1. 1PreparationUnder local anaesthetic, the tooth is shaped to make room for the crown.
  2. 2ImpressionAn impression is taken so that the crown can be custom-made.
  3. 3TemporaryA temporary crown protects the tooth while the crown is being made.
  4. 4FittingThe final crown is tried in, adjusted, then fixed in place.

Care and lifespan

You brush a crown just like a natural tooth. Floss or interdental brushes are still essential, as decay can still develop where the crown meets the tooth.

With good care and regular check-ups, a crown usually lasts many years.

Frequently asked questions

Does preparing the tooth hurt?

No. It is done under local anaesthetic. The tooth may feel slightly sensitive for a few days while you have the temporary crown.

Is a crown covered by health insurance?

A crown is partly covered by the French national health insurance (Assurance maladie). Depending on the material and the position of the tooth, some crowns are part of the French 100 % Santé scheme, under which patients with a qualifying top-up health insurance (mutuelle) have nothing left to pay.
