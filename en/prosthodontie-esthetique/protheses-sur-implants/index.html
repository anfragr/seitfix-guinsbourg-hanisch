Aesthetic prosthodontics

Implant-supported prostheses

From a single crown to a full set of teeth, fixed to or stabilised by implants.

The principle

Once the implants have fused with the bone, they act as a base for a prosthesis. The prosthesis is designed at the planning stage, so that the implants are positioned to suit the future teeth.

How implants are placed is explained in the implantology section. This page covers the prostheses they can support.

The options

  • Crown on an implantOne missing tooth, one implant, one crown. The neighbouring teeth are left untouched.
  • Implant-supported bridgeSeveral missing teeth in a row, replaced by a bridge supported by two or more implants.
  • Fixed full-arch prosthesisAll the teeth in one jaw, on four to six implants depending on the case. You do not take it out yourself.
  • Removable implant-retained dentureA denture that clips onto two to four implants. It stays firmly in place and comes out for cleaning.

Screw-retained or cemented

The prosthesis can be screwed onto the implants or cemented onto abutments (connecting pieces). A screw-retained prosthesis can be removed by your dentist for a check-up or repair. The choice depends on the situation and on where the implants are positioned.

Caring for your bridge

An implant-supported prosthesis needs cleaning every day, paying particular attention to where it meets the gum. Interdental brushes, a suitable floss or a water flosser help you reach these areas.

Regular check-ups at the practice help to detect any inflammation around the implants early.

Frequently asked questions

Can a fixed full-arch prosthesis be fitted in a single day?

In some cases, a fixed temporary prosthesis can be fitted on the same day as the implants are placed. This depends on the quality of the bone and how stable the implants are once in place. The final prosthesis is made once healing is complete.

Can I keep my old denture?

Sometimes an existing denture can be adapted to clip onto implants. Your dentist will check whether yours is suitable during the examination.
